1
SMS permission
Sirf bank ke credit SMS bheje jaate hain. OTP aur personal SMS phone se bahar nahi jaate.
Permission nahi mil rahi? Android 13+ mein: phone Settings → Apps → ApnaPay Admin → ⋮ (upar right) → Allow restricted settings. Phir yahan wapas aakar dobara Allow karein.
2
Phone link
Admin panel → Phones → is phone ka SMS forward URL. Wahan "Use this phone" dabane se ye apne aap bhar jata hai.
3
Battery saver se bachao
Warna phone app ko sula dega aur SMS late pahunchega.
  • Xiaomi/Redmi/POCO: App info → Autostart ON, Battery saver → No restrictions
  • Oppo/Vivo/Realme: App info → Battery → Allow background activity, Auto-launch ON
  • Samsung: Battery → Unrestricted
Purane payment ke SMS bhejo
Reader ON hone se pehle jo bank SMS aaye the (jaise abhi ke test payment), unhe inbox se padh kar server ko bhej dega. Sirf bank credit SMS.
