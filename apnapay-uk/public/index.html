Customers pay by UK bank transfer with a unique reference. Payments are matched automatically, your website gets a signed webhook, and you control everything from one admin panel.

Buy something, simulate the transfer, watch it turn SOLD. 🔌API & webhooksREST API with Bearer keys and HMAC-signed webhooks — the same flow your own website would use.
How a payment flows
  1. Your website calls POST /api/v1/orders and sends the customer to the payment page.
  2. The customer sends a bank transfer to your sort code + account number with reference like AP7KQ2XM.
  3. The "money in" notification is matched by reference → order Paid.
  4. Your website gets order.paid (signed) and marks the product SOLD.

This demo has no bank connection: step 3 is simulated with a button.
